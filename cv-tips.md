# 📄 CV Writing Best Practices for IT Jobs

This guide provides comprehensive tips for writing effective CVs for IT roles and strategies for
passing Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) that many companies use to filter applications.

---

## 🎯 CV Writing Best Practices for IT Roles

### 1. Tailor Your CV for Each Job

- **Use keywords from the job description** naturally throughout your CV
- Match your skills section to the required technologies mentioned
- Highlight relevant experience that aligns with the job requirements
- Customize your professional summary for each application

### 2. Start with a Strong Professional Summary

Your summary should include:

- **Years of experience** in your field
- **Primary tech stack** and specialization areas
- **Key achievements** or areas of expertise
- **Career focus** (e.g., backend development, QA automation, DevOps)

**Example:**
```
Senior Full-Stack Developer with 5+ years of experience in React, Node.js, and AWS.
Specialized in building scalable web applications and microservices architecture.
Led development teams of 3-5 engineers and improved system performance by 40%.
```

### 3. Highlight Measurable Achievements

Instead of listing duties, focus on **quantifiable results**:

- ❌ "Responsible for testing web applications"
- ✅ "Reduced bug escape rate by 35% through comprehensive automated testing suite"

- ❌ "Developed web applications"
- ✅ "Built responsive web app serving 10K+ daily users, reducing load time by 40%"

- ❌ "Managed CI/CD pipelines"
- ✅ "Implemented CI/CD pipeline reducing deployment time from 2 hours to 15 minutes"

### 4. Technical Skills Section

- **List relevant technologies** with versions where helpful (e.g., "React 18", "Java 17")
- **Organize by categories**: Languages, Frameworks, Databases, Tools, Cloud Platforms
- **Include proficiency levels** if space allows (Expert, Intermediate, Familiar)
- **Keep it current** - remove outdated technologies unless specifically required

**Example:**
```
Technical Skills:
• Languages: JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, Python, Java
• Frontend: React 18, Vue.js, HTML5, CSS3, Sass
• Backend: Node.js, Express, Django, Spring Boot
• Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
• Cloud: AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda), Docker, Kubernetes
• Tools: Git, Jenkins, Jira, Postman
```

### 5. Experience Section Best Practices

- **Use action verbs**: Developed, Implemented, Optimized, Led, Designed
- **Include project scope**: team size, timeline, budget (if relevant)
- **Mention technologies used** in each role
- **Show progression** and increasing responsibility

## 🔍 Tips for Passing ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)

### 1. Use Standard Section Headings

Stick to conventional headings that ATS systems recognize:

- ✅ **Professional Summary** or **Summary**
- ✅ **Experience** or **Work Experience**
- ✅ **Skills** or **Technical Skills**
- ✅ **Education**
- ✅ **Certifications**
- ❌ Avoid creative headings like "My Journey" or "What I Do"

### 2. Format for ATS Compatibility

- **Use standard fonts**: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman
- **Avoid tables, columns, and text boxes** - use simple linear format
- **No headers/footers** - ATS may not read them
- **Avoid images, graphics, and charts**
- **Use bullet points** instead of symbols or special characters
- **Left-align text** (avoid center or right alignment)

### 3. File Format and Naming

- **Submit as PDF** unless job posting specifically requests Word format
- **Use clear filename**: "FirstName_LastName_CV.pdf"
- **Avoid special characters** in filename

### 4. Keyword Optimization

- **Use exact phrases** from job description when they match your experience
- **Include both acronyms and full terms**: "CI/CD (Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment)"
- **Natural integration** - don't just list keywords, use them in context
- **Industry terminology** - use standard terms for your field

## 🚀 Additional Tips

### Length and Structure

- **1-2 pages maximum** for most IT roles
- **Reverse chronological order** for experience
- **Most relevant information first**
- **Consistent formatting** throughout

### Contact Information

- Include **LinkedIn profile** and **GitHub/portfolio** links
- Use **professional email address**
- **Phone number** with country code if applying internationally
- Consider including **location** (city, country) especially for remote roles

### Proofreading

- **Check for typos** and grammatical errors
- **Consistent verb tenses** (past for previous roles, present for current)
- **Test your PDF** - ensure formatting is preserved when opened on different devices
- **Get feedback** from peers in your field
