# 🧑‍🏫 Mentorship & Career Growth Resources

This document contains a curated list of high-quality mentorship websites, career coaching platforms, and online courses that help IT professionals level up their skills and advance their careers. This is especially useful for junior developers and professionals transitioning into tech.

📌 Feel free to contribute by suggesting new platforms or editing descriptions via pull request.

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Mentorship & Coaching Platforms

### General Tech Mentorship

- [MentorCruise](https://mentorcruise.com/) — 🎯 One-on-one mentorship with industry experts across all tech roles
- [ADPList](https://adplist.org/) — 🆓 Free mentorship platform connecting mentors and mentees globally
- [Coding Coach](https://codingcoach.io/) — 🆓 Free platform connecting developers for mentorship
- [Ten Thousand Coffees](https://www.tenthousandcoffees.com/) — ☕ Professional mentoring and networking platform
- [Plato](https://www.platohq.com/) — 👔 Engineering leadership mentorship and coaching
- [Stepful](https://stepful.com/) — 🎓 Mentorship for career transitions into tech

### Specialized Mentorship

- [Springboard](https://www.springboard.com/) — 🏃‍♂️ 1:1 mentorship for data science, UX, and tech careers
- [CareerFoundry](https://careerfoundry.com/) — 🎨 Mentorship for UX/UI design and web development
- [Thinkful](https://www.thinkful.com/) — 💻 Tech bootcamp with dedicated mentors
- [Lambda School (now Bloom Institute of Technology)](https://www.bloomtech.com/) — 🌱 Full-stack development with mentorship

## 📚 Online Course Platforms (Tech-Focused)

### Comprehensive Programming Platforms

- [Codecademy](https://www.codecademy.com/) — 💻 Interactive coding courses for all skill levels
- [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/) — 🆓 Free full-stack web development curriculum
- [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/) — 🆓 Free full-stack curriculum with community support
- [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/) — 🎓 University-level courses and specializations in tech
- [edX](https://www.edx.org/) — 🏛️ MIT, Harvard, and other top university tech courses
- [Udacity](https://www.udacity.com/) — 🚀 Nanodegree programs for in-demand tech skills

### Specialized Tech Skills

- [Pluralsight](https://www.pluralsight.com/) — 🔧 Technology and creative skills development
- [LinkedIn Learning](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/) — 💼 Professional development and tech courses
- [A Cloud Guru](https://acloudguru.com/) — ☁️ Cloud computing and DevOps specialization
- [Kubernetes Academy](https://kube.academy/) — 🆓 Free Kubernetes learning platform by VMware
- [MongoDB University](https://university.mongodb.com/) — 🆓 Free database and MongoDB courses

- [Design+Code](https://designcode.io/) — 🎨 UI/UX design and frontend development

### Data Science & AI

- [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn) — 🆓 Free micro-courses in data science and ML
- [Fast.ai](https://www.fast.ai/) — 🆓 Practical deep learning courses
